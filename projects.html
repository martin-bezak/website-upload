10/2026 – present · Founder and project lead

Bitcoin
Lab

Three backtested Bitcoin strategies, updated every night: what each one says today, and what $100,000 would have become in any period since 2018.

Open Bitcoin Lab

Built on eight years of hourly data. Each rule was fixed before its backtest was run, and every idea that failed is kept in the archive.

My role: the idea, which strategies to keep and which to drop, the design and features, testing and launch. The code, the statistical methods and the backtests were produced with AI.

  • MostlyBTC

    Growth

    Holds Bitcoin almost all the time and sells everything when an unusually calm stretch ends with a break downwards.

  • TrendIn/out

    Balanced

    Holds Bitcoin only above its 50-, 100- and 200-day averages, and cash earning interest otherwise.

  • SmallerRisk

    Defensive

    The same decisions as Balanced, but it holds less Bitcoin when Bitcoin is wild.

10/2026 – present · Side project, for fun

MBARCADE

A side project for fun: quick browser games on economics and markets, for students and anyone curious.

Open MB Arcade

Three games so far: Estimate it, Chart it and Sell the top. Each has a daily round, the same for everyone. More are being built.

My role: the idea and the audience, the three game concepts, the design, the three languages, testing with friends and the launch. The code and the game content were made with AI to my brief.

  • 10% off92

    Estimate it.

    750 economics and finance questions at three levels, from textbook theory to real-world numbers. Type one number; the closer you get, the more points. Plus a daily round: five questions, the same for everyone.

    Chart it.

    A real stock chart stops right at a classic pattern’s breakout. Draw the next 30 days, then see the textbook projection and what really happened.

  • 1 year20 s

    Sell the top.

    A real price chart plays one year in 20 seconds. Sell or buy back to beat buy-and-hold, then find out what you were trading: stocks, crypto, gold, oil, indices or currencies.
