    Growth

    Holds Bitcoin almost all the time and sells everything when an unusually calm stretch ends with a break downwards.

  • TrendIn/out

    Balanced

    Holds Bitcoin only above its 50-, 100- and 200-day averages, and cash earning interest otherwise.

  • SmallerRisk

    The same decisions as Balanced, but it holds less Bitcoin when Bitcoin is wild.
